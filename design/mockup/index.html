What you're looking at

Interactive mockup of RADWALLET, the Radbro-skinned privacy-first EVM wallet (MetaMask competitor). Click the big yellow tabs to flip screens, GeoCities style.

The five screens

BOOT — onboarding creed: keys local, no server, 0% fees, open source under a viral license.
HOME — accounts navigated like a webring (← Prev | Next →), balance in fat monospace, live block number rendered as a GeoCities hit counter, and a running "trackers fed today: 0" receipt.
SWAP — built-in swap with the fee table printed in full: LP fee goes to LPs, wallet fee is a struck-through 0.875% (the orange fox's cut) next to 0.00% FOREVER.
SIGN — every transaction is simulated locally and shown as plain IN/OUT lines; dangerous approvals get the They-Live billboard treatment (unlimited allowance auto-rewritten to the exact amount).
PRIVACY — the telemetry checkbox is welded ON-to-NONE because there is no server; RPC rotation, Tor routing, light-client and stealth-address roadmap marked with under-construction GIFs. All art is from the research pass (real Radbro/Radcat NFTs, real site GIFs).
