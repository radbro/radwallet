# Wallet header layout

The shared titlebar must not shrink under long content. Keep
`flex-shrink: 0` on the titlebar. Prevent its children from being clipped.

Check 372×600 popup and 520×900 docked viewports. Check Wallet and Portfolio
navigation in both browsers. Check that navigation starts after the header.

Relevant evidence is generated by the PWA, Chrome extension, and Firefox
suites. Inspect `design/app-shots/` after a fresh build. Mask secrets in
screenshots. A loaded extension must be reloaded to use rebuilt artifacts.
